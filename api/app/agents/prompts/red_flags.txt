Red-flag patterns documented in public reporting on this sector or standard in GST compliance practice — any may or may not be present:
- certified quantity beyond registered capacity or beyond verified inbound material
- inbound tonnage without corresponding weighbridge evidence
- quantities that disagree across invoice, e-way bill and weighbridge slip
- transit timings inconsistent with declared distance
- goods descriptions inconsistent with the declared HSN classification
- the same document appearing more than once
- energy use inconsistent with claimed processing
- claimed operations at a site with no visible industrial footprint

Normal and not suspicious on their own: month-to-month variation in volumes; long but feasible distances; small weighbridge differences within tolerance; documents in Hindi or other Indian languages; months with no certificates.
